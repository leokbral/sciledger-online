Use case: logo-brand.
Create a premium senior brand designer's concept presentation board for SciLedger, a real scientific publishing and peer review software platform. This is concept exploration, not a final exported logo. Based on repository audit: core is scientific publication, authors/reviewers collaborating through revision, traceable editorial records, and Hubs organizing conferences/journals/working groups. Software uses sober blue, sky blue and neutral light/dark surfaces; existing system-ui typography and a legacy Inter heading reference. Technology must not dominate the identity.
Deliver one beautifully spaced landscape board, approximately 1800x1200, warm white background, five genuinely distinct logo directions arranged three across top and two across bottom, consistent symbol scale. Large crisp flat vector-like symbols with SciLedger wordmark, generous whitespace, small unobtrusive number and concept label. Use exact case "SciLedger", refined humanist/neo-grotesk typography similar to Inter SemiBold. Name must stay a single word in one color, no SCI + LEDGER split.
Direction 01 "Registro entre pares": two substantial opposing open angular-curved strokes, complementary but separated by a generous channel, together implying an abstract S with horizontal editorial register lines. An authored, balanced square symbol, no literal chain links, no loop or infinity, no 3D ribbon. Communicates exchange between peers and continuity of a scientific record. Prefer this as a strong distinctive candidate, but show all five equally.
Direction 02 "Margem compartilhada": three offset horizontal record strokes, anchored by one deliberately shaped editorial margin. Architectural and typographic, not a literal book, page pictogram, hamburger menu or bar chart.
Direction 03 "Campo comum": asymmetrically arranged open corner shapes enclosing a shared unfilled space, intellectual commons for hubs, no central dot network, no cross, no target.
Direction 04 "Referencia viva": a single bold abstract citation-like gesture with a countershape, a substantial folded typographic punctuation form emphasizing provenance. No document outline, no checkmark or certification badge.
Direction 05 "Confluencia": three independent broad paths converging to a shared horizontal direction, flat continuous geometry, no arrows, no molecular nodes, no circuits.
Palette: primary blue #2563EB, secondary #0EA5E9 used sparingly, dark ink #111827, white. Each main symbol primarily a single blue; wordmark dark ink. Under each lockup, show tiny black symbol and favicon-scale symbol as secondary visual checks.
Constraints: outstanding professional identity quality, clean geometric negative space, thick durable silhouette, controlled curves, no shadows, gradients, mockup perspective or textures. Absolutely no atom, DNA, microscope, book, gear, brain, hexagon/blockchain icon, cubes, chains, locks, electronic circuits, university seal, globe/network node graph, ORCID-like green circular badge. No paragraphs, no marketing promises, no extra text besides brand name and the five short concept labels. Make the five forms clearly different in structure, not cosmetic variations.
